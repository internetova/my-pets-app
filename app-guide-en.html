How to use the My Pets app

A guide to the app's features

My Pets is a handy diary for looking after your animals. The app helps owners and animal volunteers keep track of their pets' health, care and important events — and forget nothing.

About the app

All data is stored on your phone only. You can always export your data and move it to another phone or share it with a friend — they can add it using the import function.

Getting started

To get started, first create a pet profile.

Filling in a profile may require some additional data — for example the pet's owner or caregiver, or the pet's category. You can add it right while creating the profile, or in advance in Settings → Reference data.

Your data and action confirmations

⚠️ All data is stored on your phone only, so please be careful when deleting records. Just in case, back up your data from time to time.

We've taken care to make sure you don't delete your data by accident.

Every sensitive action in the app requires your confirmation: deleting a record, changing the status of a vaccination or a parasite treatment, leaving an editing screen with unsaved changes. In such cases the app shows an extra window where you need to confirm the action. This mechanism is meant for deleting smaller amounts of data.

Deleting a pet

Deleting a pet works in a more elaborate way: together with the profile, every record and every file linked to that pet is deleted in an instant. They cannot be restored if you have no backup.

That is why deletion takes several steps:

  • first the app shows a bottom panel asking whether you really want to delete the pet, with a hint that the pet can be moved to the archive instead;
  • if you confirm, another panel appears where you have to enter a deletion code. The data is deleted only after the correct code is entered.

How many pets, and why categories are useful

You can add as many pets as you like.

If you have several animals, you can carry out bulk actions. Use the bulk actions button on the main Pets screen — it appears once more than one pet has been added.

If you have a lot of animals, sort them into categories. Categories are set up in the reference data and are used in filters across different parts of the app.

Why categories are useful

Ordinary owners of one to three pets don't need categories. If you don't add them in the settings, you won't see them in the app's interface either.

Categories are for people who look after the lives of many animals. As a rule these are animal volunteers with a lot of animals in their care: they rescue animals, find homes for them and follow up on how they get on.

Categories can also be useful for breeders, who need to group animals to get quick access to the records of a particular group.

Once you create categories, a filter for them appears in several places in the app:

  • the main screen with the list of animals;
  • the screen for selecting animals for bulk operations;
  • the summary screens for weight, vaccinations, parasite treatments and medications.

The Pets screen

This is the main screen for working with your pets. Here you can:

  • add new pets;
  • work with pet profiles;
  • carry out bulk actions on your pets;
  • see summary data if you have more than one pet;
  • see the current list of medications you need to give your pets.

Pet profile

The profile holds all the data about a pet. You can always edit that data — look for the buttons in the top bar of the screen, next to its title.

Editing mode gives you extra options:

  • move the pet to the archive;
  • add a date of death.

Once the profile is created, you can start adding:

  • vaccination events;
  • parasite treatment events;
  • vet visits;
  • prescription lists from the vet;
  • a photo gallery for the pet profile;
  • photos for Vet visits and Other events records;
  • the pet's weight.

There is a round + button on the profile screen — it's a shortcut for quick actions on the pet. You can perform the same actions inside the individual logs as well. Every action can be edited or deleted later.

Notes

We recommend writing down everything important in the notes, so you can find the information you need quickly.

Photo gallery

Here you can add extra photos of your pet — up to 20 photos per record.

Photos are saved in the app's internal storage. Don't forget to set up how photos are saved in Settings → Database and files → File sizes.

From the photo gallery you can share a photo or save it from the app's storage to your phone's own gallery.

Vet visits

Add vet appointments here, as well as your own observations about your pet's health and the medications it takes.

The top bar of the visit log has a + button for adding a new visit or an event related to your pet's health.

Once the record is saved, you can:

  • add photos to it;
  • fill in a prescription list with medications.

To do that, tap the visit card in the log — this takes you to the visit's detail screen. There you'll find a button for creating a prescription list and a button for adding photo documents to this visit. Photos work the same way as described in the Photo gallery section above.

Prescription list

Tap Create prescription list on the vet visit's detail screen — the Add prescriptions screen opens.

Choose a medication. The Instructions field is pre-filled with the data from the "How to use" field of the medication you selected — you can change it however you need.

Fill in the whole form. Once you've chosen a medication, an Add another medication button appears below its form. Add as many medications as you need and tap Save.

Entries in a prescription list can be edited and deleted, and new ones can be added.

The current list of medications you need to give your pet can be seen in the Medications section of the pet profile, and on the main screen with the list of pets — in the top menu, the Medications item.

Vaccination log

Holds the data on all of your pet's vaccinations, and also shows the status of the latest vaccination and the number of days until the next booster.

The top bar of the log has a + button for adding a new vaccination event.

Tap an event card to open the vaccination detail screen — it shows the details along with the description of the vaccine and your notes.

If you no longer plan to track when the next booster is due, tap Stop tracking. The vaccine is then marked as not requiring a booster, and the due-date bar is hidden.

All vaccination records can be edited and deleted.

Parasite treatments

Holds the data on all of your pet's treatments, and also shows the status of the latest treatment and the number of days until the next one.

The top bar of the log has a + button for adding a new treatment event.

Tap an event card to open the treatment detail screen — it shows the details along with the description of the product and your notes.

If you no longer plan to track when the next treatment is due, tap Stop tracking. The treatment is then marked as not requiring repetition, and the due-date bar is hidden. This is handy when, for example, you put a flea and tick collar on your pet in spring and summer but not in autumn and winter.

All treatment records can be edited and deleted.

Other events

You can add any event related to your pet to this log. Once the record is saved, you can add up to 20 photos to it and also set your pet's mood for the event — the mood is shown in the event log.

All event records can be edited and deleted.

Weight log

Add weigh-ins here. All weigh-in data is kept in this log, and the most recent weight is shown in the pet profile.

All weigh-in records can be edited and deleted.

Owner or caregiver

This is an information block showing who is responsible for this pet.

You can change the responsible person in the pet's editing mode — the edit button is in the top bar of the screen, on the right.

The owner's or caregiver's own details are edited in Settings → Reference data → Owners.

The Search screen

This screen lets you search across the whole app. To start searching, type at least 3 characters in the search field and pick the categories to search in the filter.

The Settings screen

Reference data

To use the app to the full, you'll need to fill in the reference data. You can do it in advance, or add entries as you go while working with a pet profile.

When you first installed the app, we loaded some basic entries into the reference data. You can delete the ones you don't need and add your own.

Species and breeds — a standard list that needs no further explanation.

Pet categories — we described categories above. The order of categories in the filter is changed by sorting: turn on sorting mode and drag the categories into the order you want.

Owners and caregivers — this holds the details of the people responsible for particular animals. They can be permanent owners of pets or temporary caregivers. In editing mode, an owner's or caregiver's record can be moved to the archive.

Medications — add the medications prescribed to you by vets here, as well as the ones you use on your own: parasite treatment products or vitamins, for example. The names of the medication categories cannot be deleted or edited — they are built into the app and listed alphabetically in the filter.

Vaccine types — this data is used when adding a vaccination event. Add the types of vaccination you usually give your pets. For our own cats, for example, we do rabies, a combination vaccine and leukemia.

Treatment types — this data is used when adding a parasite treatment event. Add the types of treatment you usually give your pets. For our own cats, for example, we use: flea and tick drops, a flea and tick collar, and a dewormer.

⚠️ When creating vaccine and treatment types, don't use one type for different treatments.

For example, flea and tick treatments are done with different products on different schedules: a collar and drops need to be repeated after different intervals. That's why, in our own use, these treatments are split into separate types (see the example just above). Merging them into a single general "fleas and ticks" type would be wrong.

When you add a new vaccination or treatment event, the app takes the specific type into account and marks only the previous event of the same type as superseded.

Vet clinics — add the clinics you visit with your pets here. You can also add a clinic called "Me". You'll need this list when adding a vet.

Vets — add the vets you see here. You can also add a "vet" called "Me". You'll need this list when creating a Vet visit record.

Archive

Use the archive instead of deleting. For example, if you've rehomed a pet, don't delete the pet and its new owner from the database — move them to the archive. That way you can follow how the pet is getting on and look up the data whenever you need it again.

Archived records are hidden from the active lists and don't get in the way of working with your other pets.

App settings

Here you can pick the theme that suits you best — there are several light and several dark themes.

The interface language is changed here too. Changing the language changes only the interface: all the records in the database stay in the language they were originally added in.

Database and files

Export data. Export lets you move your data to another phone, share it with a friend, or simply keep a backup just in case.

Import data. Import lets you restore the data from your backup. If you haven't exported your data for a long time, make sure you have the latest version of the app installed.

ℹ️ The phone where you restore the data from a backup must have the same version of the app as the phone where you made the backup (the export).

File sizes. Here you can set the size and quality used when photos are saved inside the app. The larger the size and the higher the quality, the more space the app takes up on your phone.
