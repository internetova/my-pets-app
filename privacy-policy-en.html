My Pets App Privacy Policy

Revision 1, effective July 11, 2026

1. Overview

My Pets (the “App”) is a pet care journal: it lets you keep pet profiles and track weight, vet visits, prescriptions, medications, and other events.

The App is developed by Tatiana Sugina (github.com/internetova) (the “Developer”).

This policy explains what data is processed when you use the App and what happens to it. The App follows the data-minimization principle: it requires no registration and never asks for your name, phone number, email address, or any other personal details.

2. Data you enter

All information in the App is created by you: pet profiles and names, photos, weight records, vet visits, prescriptions, medications, vaccinations and other events, as well as veterinary clinic and doctor details.

The App has no server of its own. All of the data listed above is stored only locally on your device and is never sent to the Developer or to any third party. The Developer has no access to this data and is technically unable to view, modify, or recover it.

3. Diagnostic data

To improve the App's stability, crash-reporting services are used: Firebase Crashlytics (Google) and AppMetrica (Yandex). When the App crashes or malfunctions, these services automatically collect technical details: the device model, operating system version, App version, and a crash log.

Diagnostic data never includes your pet records, photos, or any other information you enter into the App. Diagnostic data is processed under the rules of the respective services: the Firebase privacy policy and the Terms of Use of Yandex Metrica service and AppMetrica.

4. Device permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

  • Camera — to take a photo of your pet or a document;
  • Photos and media library — to pick an image from your gallery;
  • Storage (files) — to save App files to your phone's storage at your request, such as an export archive or a photo; only requested on older Android versions.

Permissions are used only for the purposes stated above. Captured and selected images are saved locally on your device.

5. Ads and trackers

The App contains no ads, no advertising SDKs, and no behavioral tracking tools. The diagnostic services listed in section 3 may use technical device identifiers solely to group crash reports.

6. Export and backups

You can export your data as an archive using the built-in export feature. The archive is created only at your request, is saved to a location you choose, and remains entirely under your control. The Developer does not receive a copy of the archive.

7. Security and data deletion

The App's data is kept in its protected storage on your device. Its safety also depends on your device's own security settings (screen lock, encryption, system backups).

You can delete your data at any time: remove individual records inside the App or uninstall the App from your device. Since the Developer has no access to your data, no separate deletion request is needed.

8. Children

The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children, and contains no material requiring age restrictions.

9. Changes to this policy

The Developer may update this policy. The current revision is always published on this page with its revision number and date. Significant changes are additionally mentioned in the App's release notes.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy and problem reports are welcome in the Issues section of the App's repository.
