My Pets is made by one person — Tatiana Sugina. Every message is read by the developer herself, and every message gets an answer. Get in touch if something isn't working, something isn't clear, or you'd like to see something new in the app.

My Pets is a free app with no ads and no in-app purchases. There is no sign-up and no account, and all your records are stored only on your phone: the app has no server of its own.

Where to get in touch

There are two ways — pick the one that fits your case.

  • A personal question — something happened to your records, data went missing, you can't move it to another phone, you have a question about privacy, or you simply would rather not write about it in public. Email the developer.
  • A bug, a wrong translation or an idea — something in the app works incorrectly, there's a typo in the text, or you're missing a feature. Tell us about it on GitHub: those reports are visible to other users too, and you can come back to see what happened to them.

Not sure where to write? Send an email — we'll figure it out together.

A personal question — send an email

So that the very first reply is useful, please tell us in your email:

  • what happened — in plain words, no technical details needed;
  • what you were doing before that — which screens you opened, what you tapped;
  • your phone model and system — Android or iPhone;
  • the app version — you can find it under Settings → About the App.

Feel free to attach a screenshot — it often explains the problem better than any description.

A bug, a translation or an idea — tell us on GitHub

Bug reports and suggestions are collected on the Issues page on GitHub — an open list where anyone can report a problem or suggest an improvement.

  • The app's issues page — see what's already being discussed, or create your own report.
  • How to report a bug — a step-by-step guide with screenshots, if you're doing this for the first time.

ℹ️ To send a report you need a free GitHub account. First look through the list of open issues — someone may have already reported your problem, and then it's enough to add your own comment.

⚠️ Do not include personal data in your report (your name, phone number, email, home address, etc.). The page is public and visible to everyone. If the problem can't be described without revealing personal data, send an email instead.

When to expect a reply

The developer usually replies within 2–3 business days.

If it takes longer, please check your Spam folder — the reply may have ended up there. If it's not there either, write again: emails do get lost along the way sometimes.

Replies to GitHub reports appear in the report itself, and a notification is sent to the email address of your GitHub account.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find the app version?

Open Settings → About the App — the number is shown in the "App version" row. It's worth including it in any message you send.

I deleted the app (or lost my phone). Can my records be restored?

Unfortunately, no. All data is stored only on your device — the app has no server, and the developer has no copy of your records. They can only be restored from an archive you created yourself using Data Export. So do make an archive from time to time: Settings → Database and Files → Data Export. There's more about this in the user guide.

How do I move my data to another phone?

On the old phone, run Data Export, transfer the resulting archive to the new phone in whatever way is convenient, and run Data Import there. Both phones need to have the same version of the app installed. The steps are described in the user guide.
